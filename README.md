Our planned portfolio covers clinical decision support and predictive
analytics for healthcare quality improvement in Bangladesh.

| Sequence | Track | Project | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clinical Decision Support | QCC NCD CareConnect — Visit Review Assistant | Discovery |
| 2 | Predictive Analytics | Laboratory Turnaround Delay Prediction | Planned |
| 3 | Clinical Decision Support | Discharge and Referral Review Assistant | Planned |
| 3 | Predictive Analytics | NCD Missed Follow-up Prediction | Planned |

| 4 | Clinical Decision Support | Diagnostic Test Ordering Assistant | Planned |
| 4 | Clinical Decision Support | Maternal and Newborn Care Checklist Assistant | Planned |

| 4 | Predictive Analytics | Essential Medicine or Reagent Stockout Forecast | Planned |
| 4 | Predictive Analytics | Dengue Admission and Bed-Demand Forecast | Planned |

## First milestone

QCC NCD CareConnect will review existing digital systems for hypertension
and type 2 diabetes care and verify one priority gap with an NCD care team.
The proposed Visit Review Assistant will depend on the findings.

Discovery means the team is reviewing existing systems and defining the
problem. Planned means the project is in the future work programme.

The sequence may change based on service priorities, partner readiness
